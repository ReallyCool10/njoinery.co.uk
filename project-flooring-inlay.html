The Compass Rose

Ipê Hardwood Inlay

A hardwood floor with a hand-cut compass rose inlay, worked entirely in Ipê, with boards selected and cut for tonal contrast. The compass motif is a nod to Bristol's maritime heritage. Ipê resists rot, insects and UV, so the floor is built to be walked on for decades.
