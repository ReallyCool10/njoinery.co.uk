About Us

Where Tradition Meets Precision

N Joinery creates architectural joinery and bespoke fitted furniture. We design and build kitchens, libraries, doors and other bespoke joinery and furniture elements.

We work closely with architects, interior designers, and homeowners, from first sketch through to the final fit.
