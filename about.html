About Us

Who We Are

Where Tradition Meets Precision

N Joinery is dedicated to architectural joinery and bespoke fitted furniture. We design and create each piece by hand, for kitchens, libraries, church doors and anything else that needs to be built once, correctly, for the space it's going into.

Every project starts with a conversation about the space and the brief. We work closely with architects, interior designers, and homeowners, from first sketch through to the final fit.

  • Designed and built by hand, in our own workshop
  • Bespoke designs, built to your exact specifications
  • Solid hardwoods, veneers, and specialist finishes
  • Close attention to architectural detail
  • Clear timelines and direct project management
