Accoya Doors

Church Doors

These entrance doors were commissioned for a Bristol church. We used Accoya for its dimensional stability and resistance to moisture, so the doors hold their line through wet winters without repainting. Each door has an inset blue stained glass cross, casting coloured light into the vestibule through the day.
